no.05

Cara videonya dibikinHow the videos get made

  • Proses kerjaProcess
  • 5 langkah5 steps
  • HTML + Python

Semua video di halaman ini awalnya kode HTML dan Python, terus dirender jadi file video. Urutannya kira-kira begini.Every video on this page starts as HTML and Python code and is then rendered to a video file. Roughly, it goes like this.

  1. Saya kumpulkan beberapa contoh video dengan gaya yang diinginkan, lalu saya bedah per frame, misalnya temponya atau cara teksnya muncul.I collect a few videos in a style you like and go through them frame by frame, looking at things like the pace and how the text comes in.
  2. Sebelum render, urutan adegan per detik plus 3 sampai 4 gambar contoh saya kirim dulu. Kalau mau ganti sesuatu, paling murah di tahap ini.Before anything is rendered, you get the scene order second by second and 3 to 4 sample frames. If you want something changed, this is the cheapest moment to say so.
  3. Untuk video gaya film dan stop motion, tiap frame saya gambar pakai HTML dan Python, mengikuti tampilan produknya. Video tutorial direkam langsung dari aplikasinya.For the cinematic and stop motion videos I draw every frame in HTML and Python, following the look of your product. Tutorials are recorded straight from the app.
  4. Musik dan efek suaranya juga keluar dari kode, jadi tidak perlu ngurus lisensi musik.The music and sound effects come out of code too, so there's no music licence to sort out.
  5. Terakhir, frame di tiap titik transisi saya cek satu-satu, cari teks yang kepotong, elemen yang saling numpuk, atau kontras yang kurang. Setelah beres, video final saya kirim bareng caption yang siap diposting.Last, I check the frames at every transition for cut-off text, overlapping elements and weak contrast. Then the final video goes out with a caption ready to post.

kegunaanwhat it's forRevisi tinggal ubah kodenya, terus render ulang.To revise, I change the code and render it again.
