Di balik layar: cara videonya dibuatBehind the scenes: how the videos are made

  • Proses kerjaProcess
  • 5 langkah5 steps
  • HTML + Python

Semua video di halaman ini dibuat dengan kode, bukan rekam layar atau aplikasi edit video.Every video on this page is made with code, not with screen recording or a video editor.

  1. Pelajari brief dan referensi.Study the brief and references. Saya kumpulkan beberapa video yang gayanya Anda suka, lalu saya pelajari per frame: tempo, cara teks muncul, transisi.I collect a few videos in a style you like and study them frame by frame: pacing, how the text comes in, transitions.
  2. Storyboard dan gambar contoh.Storyboard and sample frames. Sebelum render, Anda dapat urutan adegan per detik dan 3 sampai 4 gambar contoh. Revisi di tahap ini paling murah.Before rendering, you get the scene order second by second and 3 to 4 sample frames. Changes are cheapest at this stage.
  3. Bangun adegan dengan kode.Build the scenes in code. Tiap frame digambar dari kode (HTML dan Python), memakai tampilan produk Anda sendiri, bukan template.Every frame is drawn from code (HTML and Python), using the look of your own product, not a template.
  4. Musik dan efek suara.Music and sound effects. Dibuat sendiri dari kode, jadi tidak ada urusan lisensi musik.Also made from code, so there is no music licence to worry about.
  5. Cek per frame, lalu kirim.Check every frame, then deliver. Teks terpotong, tumpukan, dan kontras dicek di titik-titik transisi sebelum video final dikirim, lengkap dengan teks siap posting.Cut-off text, overlaps and contrast are checked at every transition before the final video goes out, together with a caption ready to post.

kegunaanwhat it's forRevisi cukup ubah kode, lalu render ulang.A revision means editing the code and rendering again, not starting over.
