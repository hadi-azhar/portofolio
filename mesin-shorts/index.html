Mesin Shorts

A personal automation tool by Hadi Azhar.

Mesin Shorts is a small program that runs on its owner's own computer. It assembles short vertical videos and uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channel on a schedule.

What it does

  • Builds a short video on the owner's computer (script, voice, captions, render).
  • Uploads the finished video to the owner's YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.
  • Reads basic channel information to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

Who it is for

Mesin Shorts is used only by its owner for his own channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
