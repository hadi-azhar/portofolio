Privacy Policy

Mesin Shorts · Last updated 24 October 2026

Mesin Shorts is a personal tool that runs on its owner's computer and uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channel. This page explains what Google user data it accesses and how that data is handled.

Data accessed

With the owner's permission, Mesin Shorts requests two Google permissions:

  • Manage your YouTube videos (youtube.upload): used only to upload videos the tool has created, with their title, description and tags.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): used only to read the channel name and ID, to confirm uploads go to the correct channel, and to check the status of uploaded videos.

How data is used

Data from Google is used only to provide the features above. It is not used for advertising, not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used to train AI models.

Storage

The access token Google issues is stored only on the owner's own computer. Mesin Shorts has no server and does not send YouTube data anywhere other than Google.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the stored token from the owner's computer also stops all access.

Google API Services

Mesin Shorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
